---
title: Conductor
description: A voice-driven coordinator for your agents.
---

> **Part of Pro.** Conductor is in the app, and it runs on Pro and Team, which come with Vibezeo accounts: its calls use Vibezeo's hosted voice service and are paid for in credits. The current download runs the Free plan, so it can't make calls. [The changelog](/changelog/) says what each release contains.

Conductor is a coordinator you talk to: it can start agents, prompt them, find work and report back. Click the Conductor button in the title bar or the floating ring to start or end a call, hold it to talk only while it's held, or press <kbd>Mod+Shift+Space</kbd>. A call lasts up to 30 minutes. Conductor never types into a terminal on its own say: a shell command waits for your click on screen, and a spoken yes only answers the question it was asked.
